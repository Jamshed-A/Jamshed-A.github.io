Our Heritage

A Small Workshop.
A Forty-Year Obsession.

Founded in 1985, Anfaas began as a small artisanal workshop in Grasse, France, where our founder discovered an extraordinary passion for creating fragrances that evoke emotion and memory.

From those humble beginnings we have grown into a beloved luxury perfume house — still committed to traditional craftsmanship, still blending every composition by hand, and now proudly worn across Pakistan.

Each fragrance tells a story, capturing a moment of beauty and transforming it into an olfactory experience that resonates with the soul.
